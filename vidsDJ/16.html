DJ Station Guide

📦 Global Crate & Folders

Paste a YouTube URL to populate your Singles crate. You can drag tracks into Folders to organize them. Click a folder to open it, and drag any track from a folder or singles area onto a deck to load it. Drag the vertical divider to adjust the split width.

⚡ Quickdeck Mode

Clicking Quickdeck instantly switches all active decks into DJ Mode. It hides extra buttons to give you a clean, focused turntable layout with clearly labeled VOL and SPD sliders right next to the record. The Crate remains visible so you can still drop tracks.

🖐️ Flawless Physical Scratching

Select the Hand tool. In DJ mode, the entire record spins exactly 1 rotation per song. Grabbing the vinyl instantly pauses it; turning the vinyl tracks the audio exactly 1:1 without glitching. When you let go, it instantly resumes playback.

🖍️ Crayon Markers (Hot Cues)

Select a colored crayon. Click anywhere on the linear waveform at the bottom to draw a marker. Switch back to the Hand tool and click those markers later to instantly jump (Hot Cue) to that point!
