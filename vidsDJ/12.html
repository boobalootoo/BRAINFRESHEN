DJ Station Guide

📦 Global Crate & Loading

Paste a YouTube URL or a Playlist Link into the bar at the top to populate your crate. You can drag and drop any square track from the crate directly onto a deck to load it.

⚡ Quickdeck Mode

Clicking Quickdeck instantly switches all active decks into DJ Mode and hides the crates and settings to give you a clean, focused turntable layout with embedded volume and speed sliders.

🖐️ Real Physical Scratching

Select the Hand tool. In DJ mode, the entire record and waveform ring spin exactly 1 rotation per song. Grabbing the vinyl instantly pauses it; turning the vinyl tracks the audio exactly 1:1. When you let go, it resumes playback.

🖍️ Crayon Markers (Hot Cues)

Select a colored crayon. Click anywhere on the waveform ring (DJ mode) or linear waveform (Normal mode) to draw a marker. Switch back to the Hand tool and click those markers later to instantly jump (Hot Cue) to that point!
