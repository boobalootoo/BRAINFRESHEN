DJ Station Guide

⚡ Quickdeck Mode

Clicking Quickdeck instantly switches all active decks into DJ Mode and hides all playlists, crates, and URLs to give you a clean, focused turntable mixing layout.

🎧 Modes & Playback

Normal Mode: View standard YouTube player. Clicking the linear waveform seeks through the video.

DJ Mode: The video transforms into a physical turntable. The native UI is hidden. The Tonearm moves automatically. Clicking the tonearm plays/pauses.

Autoplay & Loop: Use the checkboxes at the top of the deck to loop tracks or automatically progress through your crate.

🖐️ The Hand Tool (Absolute Skipping)

Select the Hand tool to interact with the audio. In DJ mode, clicking or dragging around the vinyl record maps directly to the track timeline. 12 o'clock is the beginning (0%), 6 o'clock is the middle (50%), and back to top is the end. Use this to instantly skip to any part of the track just by pointing!

🖍️ Crayon Markers (Hot Cues)

Select a colored crayon. Click anywhere on the waveform ring (DJ mode) or linear waveform (Normal mode) to draw a marker line. Switch back to the Hand tool and click those markers later to instantly jump (Hot Cue) to that point!

🎙️ Mic Recording Buffer

Tick the "30s Pre-buffer Mic" checkbox to make your browser continuously record the last 30 seconds of your microphone audio in the background. When you click "Record Mic", it captures that perfect moment leading up to hitting the button. Downloaded files automatically adopt the active YouTube IDs in their filename!
