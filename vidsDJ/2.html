Multi Playlist DJ Player

Play and scratch multiple videos simultaneously.

How to Use Multi DJ Player

This application allows you to load and control multiple independent video players.

Features:

  • Add Players: Click the "Add Player" button to create more independent playlist sections.
  • DJ Mode: Toggle DJ Mode on any active video. The video transforms into a record.
  • Scrubbing (Turntable): In DJ Mode, click/touch the record overlay and drag in a circle (clockwise to fast-forward, counter-clockwise to rewind).
  • Mark & Loop: Use "Mark A" and "Mark B" to isolate a section of the video. Toggle the Loop button to continuously repeat that section. Input a number to limit the iterations.
  • Volume Control: Each DJ panel has a dedicated volume slider to mix audio across players.
  • Persistent Playlists: Data is saved per-player to your local browser storage.
