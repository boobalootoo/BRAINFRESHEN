Dual Playlist Player

Play two separate playlists simultaneously side-by-side.

How to Use Dual Player

This application features two independent video players. You can watch two videos simultaneously, side-by-side.

  • Isolation: Player 1 and Player 2 have their own separate playlists and save data independently in your browser.
  • Adding Videos: Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link into the Add Video field of the player you wish to use.
  • Editing: Click on any title in the playlist to rename it.
  • Playback: Videos play inline. Click on a playlist item or press "Start" to launch the video player above the playlist.
  • Import/Export: You can backup or load CSV files per player.
